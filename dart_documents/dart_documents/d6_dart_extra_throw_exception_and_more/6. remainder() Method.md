
**`.remainder()`** method gives us a number, after taking (specified in the parameter) whatever is LEFT. Especially useful with **`Duration()`** class we can take whatever seconds are LEFT after taking minutes. Like **`.remainder(60)`**, example:

```text
140 % 60 = 20
```

So, after taking 60 two times or minutes 20 seconds are left and that is returned. But IN REALITY, it follows this, the remainder of the truncating division of **`this`** by **`other`**. The result **`r`** of this method satisfies:

```text
this == (this ~/ other) * other + r
140 == (140 ~/ 60) * 60 + 20
```

Code:

```dart
print(5.remainder(3)); // 2

print(-5.remainder(3)); // -2

print(5.remainder(-3)); // 2

print(-5.remainder(-3)); // -2
```
